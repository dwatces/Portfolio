The whole story

Read the essay

The six-year research arc behind these results, verdicts one through five.

  • Hardware

    Anyon braiding on three quantum computers

    The −1 braiding sign on the Kitaev honeycomb, reproduced on ibm_marrakesh, ibm_kingston and ibm_fez, with error bars and a matched control.

  • Theory

    The non-Abelian phase, end to end

    Chern number ±1, a chiral Majorana edge mode and Majorana zero modes bound to vortices, then braided numerically.

  • Error correction

    Symmetry-tied neural decoders

    At the same parameter count, about 4× more sample-efficient, and level with the matching decoder (0.9943 against 0.9948).

  • Geometry

    When the lattice owns the pattern

    Square against hexagonal lattices at matched density: no difference at normal scales, until below about 3 lattice constants. 11,946 patterns.

  • Real data

    The equivariance law on city data

    New-station demand forecasting in five cities over two seasons: the hex-equivariant model wins 10 of 10.

  • Quantum ML

    Equivariant readouts for quantum ML

    A quantum reservoir with an exactly symmetry-tied readout gains the most where data is scarce.

  • Materials

    Match the symmetry, exactly

    Classifying Chern phases of the Haldane model: the matched C3 prior wins at every scarce size, and the too-large C6 group does worse.

  • Index

    All research results

    Every write-up in one place, each from a completed, logged run.

All the results are on the research index, and the code and logs are on GitHub.
