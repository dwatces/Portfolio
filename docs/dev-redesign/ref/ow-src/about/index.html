Daniel Olliver · Auckland

The designer and developer behind Olliver Web.

I design and build websites and web applications: the screens, the backend and the integrations. Olliver Web is just me, so the person you talk to first is the one who builds it.

Tell me about your project

From the interface to the database.

I’m the Auckland full stack developer behind Olliver Web, my web design and development service, building custom websites, online stores and the software behind them. My everyday tools are React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js and Supabase. I work virtually by default and can meet Auckland clients at their business by appointment. My wider work includes a patient-assessment platform for a UK healthcare provider, Stripe payment integrations on client projects, and, at 3PM in Auckland (2022–2024), live scheduling software used by construction trades.

In all, I’ve spent about four years building for the web, and I’ve been freelance since 2024. I have a BSc in Computer Science from AUT and a Postgraduate Certificate in AI from the University of Auckland, and I’ve built retrieval-based (RAG) document-AI prototypes. AI goes into a client project only where it earns its place, scoped and tested like any other integration.

So a business website gets the same care from me as software: it has to look right, load quickly, keep any search visibility it already has and still make sense to whoever runs it after launch.

What that looks like in practice.

Direct

You work with the developer doing the work. Scope, cost and trade-offs don’t get filtered through a sales layer.

Evidence-led

Performance, accessibility and migration claims are tested. If a simpler option fits better, I’ll say so.

Yours to own

Your domain, hosting, analytics and content sit in accounts held in your name. At launch I hand over the agreed code, and any platform subscription is explained up front.

Public client work and Google reviews

Back to Nature Tours is a live Dunedin tourism rebuild on Webflow CMS, which the owner can edit himself, with FareHarbor booking built into the site and an SEO migration that kept or redirected most of the old page addresses. Olliver Web’s Google Business Profile is linked directly so reviews can be checked at the source.
